Vision

In the next decade, Headwaters College envisions to become one of the premiere institutions that caters 21st century education commiting the promotion of the quality of life through enhancement of skills, knowledge, and attitude.

Mission

1. Nurture students towards piety, patriotism, and prestige.
2. Educate students to be true Filipinos - law-abiding citizens.
3. Provide students with competitive and conducive learning environments by providing state-of-the-art facilities, up-to-date learning materials, highly-competitive educators and secured vicinity.
4. Strengthen students' attitude towards the community; enhance students' competence in both technical and life-long skills, and update students' knowledge using the 21st century learning approaches.
5. Enforce continuous improvements through evaluation of school performances in different services.
6. Continue the community engagement as part of the organizational social responsibility.
